🌦️ Where could it go? The weather forecast

Nobody can predict Bitcoin. But we can look back and ask: after planting some money on a random day in the past, what did it look like later? The answer is a range, like a weather forecast’s “chance of rain” — not a promise.

What do these shaded areas mean?

The lighter, wider area shows the middle 80% of outcomes from past Bitcoin price movements. The darker area shows the middle 50%. The line marks the middle historical outcome. These are examples from the past, not limits on what Bitcoin could do next; the real future could be much better or worse.

⚙️ How this is worked out (optional)

We take every past day since the chosen start year as a pretend “planting day” and see what the money was worth after your chosen time. We then show where most of those results landed. Planting days close together overlap, so treat it as a rough guide.

Bitcoin’s earliest years had huge jumps that may never repeat, so older history can look rosier than it should.

This is a history lesson, not a prediction. Past results are no guide to the future, and Bitcoin can lose most of its value. The range ignores fees and tax and is not financial advice.
