⚙️ Your growing rules

A dip is measured from the last buy reference price (initially the first observed price). Each purchased lot gets its own rebound target. Fee is an adjustable estimate, not a live Kraken fee quote.

⏸ Paused — no simulated trading

🧪 How it works

Example: BTC falls 5% → the grower buys £10 worth (minus fees). If that purchased portion later gains 10% from its buy price, it sells half and leaves half growing. New dips can create new lots. It won't borrow money or sell Bitcoin you never bought.

At £10 order sizes, harvesting half may fall below Kraken's live minimum order. This is a learning prototype, not an executable strategy. It does not handle slippage, tax or exchange minimums.
